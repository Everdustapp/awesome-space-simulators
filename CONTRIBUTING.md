# Contributing

Thanks for helping. To add or fix an entry, open a pull request or an issue.

An entry should:

- Be a space, astronomy or physics simulator, planetarium, sky map or a tool people use with them.
- Link to the project's own website, repository or store page. No affiliate or tracking links.
- Say what it does, where it runs and what it costs, from its own website or store page. Note whether it simulates (objects move under physics) or shows (catalogs or models you fly through) when that isn't obvious.
- Be described plainly. No "best", "ultimate" or marketing copy.

Format:

```
- [Name](https://link) - What it does. Simulates or shows. Price. Platforms.
```

Prices are US list prices. If a price or platform has changed, a correction is just as welcome as a new entry.

This list is maintained by Everdust (Decosa, Inc.), which makes one of the apps listed. Entries for other apps are judged the same way as ours.
