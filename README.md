# Awesome Space Simulators [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of space and physics simulators, planetariums and astronomy apps, on desktop, in the browser, on phones and in headsets.

Each entry says what it does, where it runs and what it costs. Prices are US list prices and were checked in November 2026. They change, so check before you buy.

**Simulate or show?** Some apps *simulate*: objects move under gravity, so orbits change and galaxies can collide. Others *show*: real catalogs or models you fly through, where nothing pulls on anything at large scale. Both are useful. The lists below say which is which where it matters.

> Disclosure: this list is maintained by [Everdust](https://everdust.ai) (Decosa, Inc.), which makes one of the apps below. Everdust is listed in the same format as everything else, and we say plainly where another app is the better pick. Corrections and additions are welcome.

## Universe sandboxes and 3D universes (desktop)

- [Universe Sandbox](https://universesandbox.com/) - Physics-based space sandbox by Giant Army. Collide planets, build star systems, change gravity, merge galaxies. Simulates. Windows and macOS on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/230290/Universe_Sandbox/), $29.99. PC VR support [ended with Update 35](https://universesandbox.com/blog/2025/02/future-of-vr-on-universe-sandbox/) in 2025.
- [SpaceEngine](https://spaceengine.org/) - A 1:1 scale universe built from star and galaxy catalogs, with uncharted space filled in procedurally. Fly from a planet's surface to a galaxy billions of light-years away. Shows. Windows and PC VR on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/314650/SpaceEngine/), $29.99.
- [Celestia](https://github.com/CelestiaProject/Celestia) - Real-time 3D space simulation: visit planets, moons, star clusters and galaxies from any point in space and time. Shows. Free and open source (GPL-2.0). Windows, macOS, Linux, plus mobile and Vision Pro versions (see below).
- [Gaia Sky](https://gaiasky.space/) - Real-time 3D astronomy visualization from Heidelberg University's Astronomisches Rechen-Institut, built within ESA's Gaia mission. Loads real catalogs such as Gaia's. Shows. Free and open source (MPL-2.0). Windows, macOS, Linux, OpenXR headsets.
- [OpenSpace](https://www.openspaceproject.com/) - Open-source interactive astrovisualization software used by planetariums, museums, classrooms and researchers. Shows. Free. Windows, macOS, Linux.
- [Mitaka](https://4d2u.nao.ac.jp/mitaka/) - Viewer from the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan's 4D2U project, from Earth out to the large-scale structure of the universe, using observational data and models. Shows. Free. Windows only.
- [Everdust for Mac](https://everdust.ai/mac) - Ready-made live simulations, such as colliding galaxies and a black hole bending its own light, that can move with the music your Mac is playing. Simulates, but it is not a build-your-own sandbox: for that, pick Universe Sandbox. Free download, Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later.

## Planetariums and sky maps

- [Stellarium](https://stellarium.org/) - The best-known free desktop planetarium: a realistic sky for any place and time. Free and open source (GPL-2.0). Windows, macOS, Linux.
- [KStars](https://kstars.kde.org/) - Feature-rich desktop planetarium from KDE, with telescope and imaging tools for observers. Free and open source. Windows, macOS, Linux.
- [Cartes du Ciel (SkyChart)](https://www.ap-i.net/skychart/) - Star charting software for planning observations. Free software under the GNU GPL. Windows, macOS, Linux.
- [Stellarium Mobile](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stellarium-mobile-star-map/id1458716890) - Phone planetarium from Noctua Software, by the original creator of Stellarium. Point your phone at the sky to identify stars, planets and satellites. Free with paid extras. iPhone, iPad, [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noctuasoftware.stellarium_free).
- [Sky Map](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.stardroid) - Originally Google Sky Map, now [open source](https://github.com/sky-map-team/stardroid). Identify stars, planets and constellations by pointing your phone. Free, no ads. Android.
- [SkySafari](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skysafari/id1257281849) - Observing planner and sky chart many amateur astronomers use at the telescope. $4.99, optional Premium subscription. iPhone, iPad, [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.simulationcurriculum.skysafari7).
- [Star Walk 2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/star-walk-2-plus-live-sky-map/id1112481571) - Star map with AR, a time machine and 3D constellation figures. Free with ads and an optional subscription; [Star Walk 2 Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/star-walk-2-pro-sky-map-live/id892279069) is a one-time purchase. iPhone, iPad, Android.
- [SkyView Lite](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skyview-lite/id413936865) - Point-and-identify AR star finder. Free. iPhone, iPad, [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.t11.skyviewfree).
- [Sky Guide](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sky-guide/id576588894) - Hold it up to name stars, constellations, planets and satellites, with an AR mode and Space Station pass alerts. Free with Plus and Pro subscriptions. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro.
- [Night Sky](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/night-sky/id475772902) - Star map by iCandi Apps that identifies stars, planets, constellations and satellites. Free with subscriptions. iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro.

## In a web browser

- [NASA's Eyes](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/) - NASA's suite of 3D apps built on real NASA data: the solar system, exoplanets, Earth satellites and missions. Free.
- [Stellarium Web](https://stellarium-web.org/) - Stellarium's planetarium in the browser. Free.
- [WorldWide Telescope](https://worldwidetelescope.org/webclient/) - Sky survey imagery and a 3D universe in the browser, now an open-source project ([engine on GitHub](https://github.com/WorldWideTelescope/wwt-webgl-engine), MIT). Free.
- [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/) - Real-time 3D model of the solar system and night sky. Free in the browser, with apps for [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solar-system-scope/id863969175) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=air.com.eu.inove.sss2).
- [Space Radar](https://www.spaceradar.ai/) - Live 3D map of space from public data: about 17,000 satellites where they are now, the planets and moons, 2.5 million stars in 3D, the ground at Moon and Mars landing sites, the sky from your street and narrated guided trips. Open source ([MIT, on GitHub](https://github.com/Sara-Managed-Projects/space-radar)). Shows. Free, no account. Any modern browser, phones included.
- [PhET Gravity and Orbits](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/gravity-and-orbits) - Move the Sun, Earth, Moon and space station and watch the orbits change. Simulates. Free, from the University of Colorado Boulder.
- [PhET My Solar System](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/my-solar-system) - Set positions, velocities and masses and watch the "gravitational ballet". Simulates. Free.
- [100,000 Stars](https://stars.chromeexperiments.com/) - Chrome Experiment mapping over 100,000 nearby stars in 3D, colored by temperature. Shows. Free.
- [galaxy-sim](https://andrewdcampbell.github.io/galaxy-sim/) - Open-source WebGL N-body galaxy simulation from a student graphics project. Simulates. Free. Expect it to be rough on phones.
- [Everdust in the browser](https://everdust.ai/play) - 27 free live scenes on WebGL 2, including a [galaxy collision](https://everdust.ai/play/galaxy-collision), a black hole, a gravity sandbox and a solar system simulator. Simulates. Free, no install.
- [NASA Space Place: Play](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/menu/play/) - Free space games and activities for kids, from NASA.

## Spaceflight and orbital mechanics

- [Kerbal Space Program](https://www.kerbalspaceprogram.com/) - Build spacecraft from parts and fly them with aerodynamic and orbital physics. The game that taught many people orbital mechanics. Windows, macOS, Linux on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/220200/Kerbal_Space_Program/), $39.99.
- [Principia](https://github.com/mockingbirdnest/Principia) - Kerbal Space Program mod that replaces its simplified orbits with full N-body gravity. Free and open source (MIT).
- [Orbiter](https://github.com/orbitersim/orbiter) - Long-running space flight simulator with realistic Newtonian physics, now open source (MIT). Free. Windows. Community at [Orbiter Forum](https://www.orbiter-forum.com/).
- [Juno: New Origins](https://www.simplerockets.com/) - Build rockets, planes and cars from parts and test them in realistic physics, from the ground to orbit. Windows and macOS on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/870200/Juno_New_Origins/), $19.99; also on [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jundroo.junoneworigins) (free with ads, or a paid Complete Edition).
- [Spaceflight Simulator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.StefMorojna.SpaceflightSimulator) - Build a rocket from parts and fly it to orbit, the Moon or Mars, with realistically scaled planets. Free with in-app purchases on Android; $14.99 on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1718870/Spaceflight_Simulator/).

## Gravity sandboxes on phones and tablets

- [Milky Way: Cosmic Playground](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.SciXP.CosmicCollisions) - Gravity sandbox that merges whole galaxies in 3D, including a Milky Way and Andromeda collision, with SPH for collisions. Simulates. Free with in-app purchases. Android, [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/milky-way-cosmic-playground/id1607487131).
- [Pocket Galaxy](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.co.pocketlabs.pocketuniverse) - Spawn planets and stars under real gravity, then heat, freeze or smash them. Simulates. $2.49 on Android (a free version has ads), $3.99 on [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocket-galaxy-space-sandbox/id1214937203).
- [Gravity Simulator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.testtubegames.gsim) - Launch stars and planets, then change the law of gravity itself or switch on general relativity. Simulates. $3.99, no ads. Android.
- [Particle Physics Simulator](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tss.android) - N-body sandbox: every particle pulls on every other, with adjustable gravity, mass and collisions. Simulates. Free. Android.
- [Solar Smash](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.paradyme.solarsmash) - Planet destruction game with a Solar System Smash mode for building systems and testing collisions. Free with ads and in-app purchases. Android, [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/solar-smash/id1495723103).
- [Galaxy Merge: Space Sandbox](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/galaxy-merge-space-sandbox/id6780233315) - Calm touch sandbox: fling planets, stars and comets together and they merge up to neutron stars and black holes. Free. iPhone, iPad.
- [Everdust](https://everdust.ai) - Ready-made live simulations to touch: a galaxy collision of up to about a million stars, black holes that bend light, physics games and narrated science stories. Simulates. Free, no ads; optional Everdust Plus, and The Story of Everything is free in full. [iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/everdust-space-science-games/id6818004038), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.decosa.everdust).

## VR, AR and spatial computing

- [Titans of Space PLUS](https://www.drashvr.com/titans-of-space/) - Classic guided VR tour that shows the sizes of planets, moons and giant stars. $9.99 on [Meta Quest](https://www.meta.com/experiences/titans-of-space-plus/2359857214088490/).
- [Stellarium VR](https://www.meta.com/experiences/stellarium-vr/6628847520574290/) - Stellarium's planetarium in a headset. $9.99. Meta Quest.
- [Star Chart](https://www.meta.com/experiences/star-chart/1951863938215666/) - Real-time night sky, a 3D solar system and short scenes on the Moon, Mars and the ISS. $9.99. Meta Quest.
- [Mission: ISS](https://www.meta.com/experiences/mission-iss/2094303753986147/) - Hands-on simulation of life on the International Space Station: move in zero gravity, dock a capsule, go on a spacewalk. Free. Meta Quest.
- [Celestia XR](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/celestia-xr/id6476618172) - Celestia built for Apple Vision Pro: fly to any object in its database. Free.
- [Caelum: Spatial Planetarium](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/caelum-spatial-planetarium/id6757993013) - Planetarium for Apple Vision Pro with stars at their real positions for your place and time. Free to download.
- [Everdust](https://everdust.ai) - Reach into a galaxy collision or a spinning black hole with your hands, play hand-tracked physics games, and on Vision Pro (since 1.1) look at a planet or nebula in the real night sky and pinch to fly there. Simulates. Free, no ads; optional Everdust Plus. [Apple Vision Pro](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/everdust-space-science-games/id6818004038), [Meta Quest 3 and 3S](https://www.meta.com/experiences/everdust-space-art-worlds-minigames/1383098364880106/).
- SpaceEngine and Gaia Sky (above) also run in PC headsets.

## Research simulation codes

Open-source codes astronomers use for real simulations. Not apps: expect a compiler and a cluster.

- [REBOUND](https://github.com/hannorein/rebound) - N-body integrator for planetary dynamics, with a Python interface. GPL-3.0.
- [GADGET-4](https://wwwmpa.mpa-garching.mpg.de/gadget4/) - Parallel cosmological N-body and SPH code for structure formation and galaxy evolution.
- [SWIFT](https://swift.strw.leidenuniv.nl/) - Task-based cosmological hydrodynamics and gravity code ([GitHub](https://github.com/SWIFTSIM/SWIFT), LGPL-3.0).
- [AREPO](https://arepo-code.org/) - Public version of the moving-mesh magnetohydrodynamics code used for cosmological simulations.
- [galpy](https://docs.galpy.org/) - Python package for galactic dynamics: orbit integration in galactic potentials. BSD-3-Clause.
- [yt](https://yt-project.org/) - Python toolkit for analyzing and visualizing the output of astrophysical simulations.

## Data and ephemerides

- [JPL Horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/) - NASA JPL's service for precise positions of solar system bodies at any time.
- [HYG Database](https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database) - Open star catalog combining Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star and Gliese data.
